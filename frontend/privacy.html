Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your agricultural data.

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

1. Introduction

At CropAI, we take your privacy seriously. We understand that your farm data, soil reports, and yield information are valuable assets. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our crop recommendation services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you register for an account, request a crop recommendation, or contact our support team:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and farm location.
  • Agricultural Data: Soil type, pH levels, moisture levels, historical yield data, and crop preferences.
  • Uploaded Content: Images of crops or soil for analysis.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide accurate AI-driven crop recommendations tailored to your specific conditions.
  • To improve our machine learning models (data is anonymized before finding patterns).
  • To send you important weather alerts or pest warnings relevant to your region.
  • To maintain and improve the security of our platform.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may employ third-party companies to facilitate our service (e.g., cloud hosting, email delivery), but they are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose usage data where required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. All sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can manage your profile settings directly through the dashboard or contact our support team for assistance with data export or deletion requests.
