Our Story

Cultivating the Future of Agriculture

Founded in 2024, CropAI began with a simple mission: to empower every farmer with the data insights previously available only to large-scale operations.

Using advanced machine learning and affordable smart sensors, we've helped over 10,000 farmers across the globe increase their yields while reducing water usage and environmental impact. Our team of agronomists and data scientists works tirelessly to refine our algorithms for local climates and crop varieties.
