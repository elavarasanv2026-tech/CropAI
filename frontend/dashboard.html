Soil Testing Guide & Collection Process
How to Collect soil Samples
1

Remove surface waste and grass from the sampling spot.

2

Dig soil in a V-shape up to 15–20 cm depth.

3

Collect soil from 5–10 different spots in one acre.

4

Mix all samples thoroughly and remove stones/roots.

5

Take about 500g–1kg as the final sample.
